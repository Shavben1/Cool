# Mealify

Mealify is a responsive single-page web app that helps you plan meals around the ingredients you already have at home. Type or tap pantry items, hit **Find meals**, and instantly get curated recipes complete with quick prep steps and a shopping list for anything you still need to buy.

## Features

- **Ingredient-driven suggestions** – Enter your own ingredients or tap common pantry chips to build your kitchen inventory.
- **Smart matching** – Mealify surfaces recipes ranked by how many of your ingredients they use, so you cook what you already have.
- **Quick prep guides** – Each suggestion includes a short set of actionable steps to take you from pantry to plate fast.
- **Dynamic shopping list** – Missing items across all suggested meals are combined into one tidy list so you know exactly what to pick up.
- **Responsive design** – Works beautifully on phones, tablets, and desktops with thoughtful spacing and accessible controls.

## Getting started

Open `index.html` in your browser or serve the project locally:

```bash
python -m http.server
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000` to try Mealify.

## Customization

- Modify the curated recipes inside the `<script>` block in `index.html` to tailor Mealify to your taste or dietary needs.
- Adjust colors, spacing, or typography in `styles.css` to fit your brand.
